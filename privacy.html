Privacy Policy — Lost Archive Uploader

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Lost Archive Uploader is a private, single-user tool operated by the owner of the YouTube channel "The Lost Archive" (الأرشيف المفقود). It is not offered to the public.

What data the app accesses

The app uses Google OAuth to access the owner's own YouTube account with the scopes youtube.upload and youtube, only to upload videos, set thumbnails, and schedule publishing on the owner's channel.

How data is used and stored

  • The OAuth token is stored only as an encrypted secret in the owner's private repository and is used solely to upload the owner's own videos.
  • No data from any other user is collected, stored, sold, or shared.
  • The app does not read viewers' data, comments, or personal information.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube API usage is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account → Third-party connections.

Contact

Through the channel: youtube.com/@thelostarchive_ar
